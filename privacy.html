Who we are

Design & Deploy is a digital design and development studio based in Noida, India. For any question about this policy or your information, write to support@designandeploy.in.

Information we collect

  • What you give us. When you use the project enquiry form we receive your name, email address and message, and, if you provide them, your phone number, company or brand, the service you need, your timeline and your budget. We also receive anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information. Our website host, Hostinger, automatically records standard server information such as IP address, browser type, device and the pages requested, so the site can be delivered and kept secure. The notification email we receive when you submit the form also includes the time and IP address of the submission.

We do not take payments on this site and we do not ask you to create an account.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals.
  • To deliver the services you hire us for.
  • To keep business records.
  • To prevent spam and abuse, for example by limiting how many times a form can be submitted from one IP address.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Your consent

You choose to share information when you submit the form or contact us, and we use it only for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use trusted providers to run our business: Hostinger for website hosting and email, Google Fonts for the fonts on our pages (which means your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads), and WhatsApp (Meta) if you message us there. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set tracking or advertising cookies on this website and we do not currently run analytics. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

Links to other sites

Our social media links lead to platforms that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver any project and keep business records. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, unless we need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections and limited access to our mailbox, but no online service is completely secure.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw your consent, or raise a complaint about how we have handled your information. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. These rights are in line with Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is not aimed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions or requests? Write to support@designandeploy.in. You can also read our Terms of Service.

This policy describes how this website works today. If we add new tools, such as analytics, we will update it first.
