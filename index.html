About Design & Deploy

Two specialists. One digital studio.

Design & Deploy was built around a simple idea: great digital work happens when design and development work together from the beginning.

We're a two-person digital studio combining creative direction, graphic design, UI/UX and web development to help businesses turn ideas into polished digital experiences.

No giant agency structure. No unnecessary layers. Just direct collaboration and focused execution.

We're based in Noida, India, helping businesses across Delhi-NCR, India and foreign build stronger brands and better digital experiences.

Why we started

Too often, businesses have to choose between a design team that understands aesthetics and a development team that understands technology. We don't think you should have to.

Design & Deploy brings both disciplines together from the beginning. The result is a more connected process, faster communication and websites where the design and technology actually work together.

Our philosophy

Design with purpose.

We don't design for decoration. Every visual decision should communicate something.

Build with intention.

Technology should serve the experience, not complicate it.

Keep things simple.

The best solutions are often the ones that make complex things feel effortless.

Sweat the details.

Typography, spacing, motion, responsiveness, loading speed. The small things add up.

Keep moving.

Ideas are valuable. Execution is what turns them into businesses.

Built for Better Collaboration.

Great work happens when ideas move without unnecessary barriers. We work closely with our clients, giving you direct access to the people shaping and executing your project. That means faster communication, sharper decisions, and a seamless path from concept to execution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Design & Deploy do?

Design & Deploy is a digital design and development studio specializing in website design, web development, UI/UX design, branding, graphic design and e-commerce development.

Where is Design & Deploy based?

Design & Deploy is a digital studio based in India, working with businesses and clients across India and foreign.

Do you design and develop websites?

Yes. We handle the complete process from website strategy and UI/UX design through development, testing and deployment.

Do you work with WordPress?

Yes. We work with WordPress and WooCommerce as well as custom front-end development and headless WordPress solutions, depending on the project's requirements.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can audit an existing website and redesign its visual system, user experience, structure and technical implementation.

Do you provide branding services?

Yes. Our branding services include logo design, visual identity, brand systems, guidelines, marketing creatives and digital brand assets.

Do you work with startups?

Absolutely. We work with startups and growing businesses that need a strong brand and digital presence without unnecessary complexity.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on the scope, number of pages, functionality, integrations, design requirements and development complexity. Contact us with your requirements and we'll recommend the right approach.

How long does it take to build a website?

Project timelines depend on complexity and scope. A focused business website can take considerably less time than a custom platform or e-commerce project. We'll provide a timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you maintain my website after launch?

Yes. We can provide ongoing website maintenance, updates, improvements and technical support depending on your requirements.
